Proposal: a side-rung admission rule

Status: proposal 2026-09-18; adopted 2026-09-24. The 180, 360 and 600 s batch was admitted together with 9 and 90 s (the 9-10 s family’s chain), in the pyramids paper’s revised ladder of 16 main and 9 side rungs; see pogs.qmd. The ladder below this line is the pre-adoption one (13 spine rungs, 6 side rungs) and is kept as the record of the decision. This document states the rule that decides whether a resolution becomes a side rung or is resampled on entry, records what that decision costs, and applies it to the case that triggered it.

The trigger

Converting WorldClim 2.1 at 10 arcminutes (wc2.1_10m_prec_*.tif) with make_paths_pogs_in_parallel fails:

make_path_pog cannot infer an output resolution ... its cell size is 0.16666667 degrees
(600 arcseconds), which is not one of the supported pyramid resolutions
(0, 0, 0, 0, 1, 3, 10, 15, 30, 150, 300, 900, 1800, 3600, 18000, 36000, 108000, 324000, 648000).
Suggested output_arcseconds=900: the closest supported resolution at or coarser than the
native cell, so no detail is invented. Use 300 to upsample instead.

WorldClim 2.1 is distributed at four tiers: 30 s, 2.5′, 5′ and 10′. Three of them are rungs (30, 150, 300 s). The fourth, 600 s = 1/6°, is not. The two exits the message offers are both wrong for this file, and the message itself is wrong in two places (see What the error message gets wrong).

What the code enforces today

Three mechanisms in hazelbean/pyramids.py decide everything about a rung:

  1. Admissibility. pyramid_side_arcseconds is asserted at import: every side rung must be an integer divisor of at least one spine rung, tested as reduced fractions. Nothing else is checked.
  2. Overview derivation. A rung’s overview levels are every coarser rung, spine or side, that is an integer multiple of it. The table is not written; it is computed.
  3. Publication. Every rung gets a tile-matrix entry (get_pyramid_tile_matrix_set) and a tile-edge entry (pyramid_tile_degrees).

Mechanism 1 makes admission nearly free: any grid of 1/n° divides the 1° rung. Mechanism 2 is what makes admission expensive, and it is the issue this proposal formalizes:

Admitting a rung R adds one overview level to every existing rung that divides R. Every POG at those rungs then fails is_path_pog (overviews are matched by exact dimensions) and must be regenerated.

An admission is therefore a lattice-wide event, never a local one. The manuscript’s criterion, “join height, not divisibility, is the binding constraint”, is prose: no code expresses a join-height bound, a consumption rung, or the churn a candidate would cause.

The rule

A candidate resolution R becomes a side rung only if it passes all four tests, in order.

  1. Admissible. R is rational and divides at least one spine rung. Mechanical; already asserted at import. The smallest spine rung R divides is its join rung J.
  2. Owned. A named product family is distributed natively at R on a lat/lon grid. No owner, no rung. This is why 2° and 4° were removed and why 30°, 90°, 180° exist only as structural bridges.
  3. Served. Every rung at which the devstack consumes the family is either R itself or at or above J. Below J, reading the family at a spine rung is a non-block resample whether or not R is a rung, so the rung buys native storage only, and resample-on-entry to the consumption rung is equivalent. A family that fails this test is entered by resampling and recorded in the entry table (A.1 of the manuscript; the “Products with no lat/lon grid” table in pogs.qmd).
  4. Batched. Admissions are made in a batch aligned with a base_data regeneration, never singly, because of the churn in mechanism 2. Each batch records, per admitted rung, the finer rungs whose chains changed.

Test 3 is the manuscript’s “join height” criterion made checkable. Tests 2 and 4 are the parts the manuscript does not state.

The cost of a candidate, computed

For each candidate, the chains that change if it is admitted to the current lattice (computed from the live derivation, 2026-09-18):

600 s (1/6°) WorldClim 2.1 10′ 1800, 3600, … 1800 s 3, 6, 30, 60, 180, 540, 1080 10: 1-9, 3-10, 1-3, 1, 3, 10, 15, 30, 150 (+4), 300 (+2) 300 s POGs grow ~25 % (a factor-2 level); 150 s ~6 %
180 s (0.05°) CHIRPS, MODIS CMG 900, 1800, … 900 s 5, 10, 20, 100, 200, 600, 1800, 3600 9: 1-9, 3-10, 1-3, 9-10, 1, 3, 10, 15, 30 (+6) 30 s POGs grow ~3 %
360 s (0.1°) ERA5-Land 1800, 3600, … 1800 s 5, 10, 50, 100, 300, 900, 1800 9: 1-9, 3-10, 1-3, 9-10, 1, 3, 10, 15, 30 (+12) 30 s POGs grow <1 %
9000 s (2.5°) GPCC, legacy reanalyses 18000, … 18000 s 2, 4, 12, 36, 72 13: everything from 1-9 s to 1800 s negligible per file, but touches every rung

Note that 600 s does not divide 900 s (ratio 1.5) and 9/10 s does not divide 600 s, so the WorldClim 10′ tier joins the spine at half a degree, one rung higher than CHIRPS does.

Applying the rule

600 s, WorldClim 10′. Admissible; owned; served, because the 10′ tier is the one used where half-degree and one-degree grids are the target, and its join is 1800 s. It has one property no other candidate has: 600 is an integer multiple of 300, 150 and 30, so with it admitted the four WorldClim tiers nest as exact block sums (30 → 150 → 300 → 600 by factors 5, 2, 2). Without it the family is split, three tiers native and one resampled. Verdict: admit.

180 s, CHIRPS and MODIS CMG. Admissible; owned; join 900 s, which is the LUH2 and GTAP-AEZ rung, so it is served for Earth-economy work (not for SEALS covariates at 30 s or 300 s, which get a resample either way). The manuscript currently lists it as resample-on-entry because it “joins too high”. That position is not consistent with admitting 600 s, whose join is higher. Verdict: admit in the same batch as 600 s, or reject both.

360 s, ERA5-Land. Admissible; owned by the dominant reanalysis; same join as 600 s. Verdict: same decision as 600 s.

9000 s, 2.5°. Admissible, but no devstack consumer, and it churns thirteen rungs. Verdict: reject; resample on entry.

Recommendation

Admit 180, 360 and 600 s as one batch, timed with the base_data regeneration that the 2026-09-16 overview-rule change already requires, so the regeneration happens once. The cost is concentrated in one place, 300 s POGs gaining a factor-2 overview (about a quarter more overview bytes on files that are 4,320 × 2,160 cells, so megabytes), and the benefit is that the three most-used coarse climate families in the stack become native rungs with exact paths to the spine. The alternative, admit none and enter all three by resampling, is coherent too; what is not coherent is the manuscript’s current state, which admits WorldClim’s finer tiers and rejects its coarsest on a criterion that would also reject nothing else on the list.

Either way, the lattice after this batch is 13 spine + 9 side = 22 matrices, or stays at 19.

What the error message gets wrong

Recorded here for the fix, not fixed in this proposal.

  • Side rungs print as 0, 0, 0, 0. _explain_unsupported_resolution in hazelbean/pog.py formats every supported rung with int(), so the four fractional rungs print as zero. It should use hb.arcseconds_to_token.
  • The lossy and lossless exits are labelled backwards for this file. The message calls 900 s the choice where “no detail is invented” and 300 s an “upsample”. For a 600 s input, 600 → 900 is a ratio of 1.5: a resample that mixes cells and cannot be undone. 600 → 300 is an integer refinement by 2: nearest-neighbour replication invents nothing, and a block sum back to 600 s recovers the input exactly. The suggestion rule should be: prefer the coarsest rung that divides the native cell (lossless refinement); else the finest rung the native cell divides (exact aggregation); else the nearest coarser rung, and say that this last case is a true resample.
  • The entry table is prose only. The manuscript’s A.1 says resample-on-entry products are “recorded in an entry table kept beside the rung table”. No such table exists in code; a small registry (product, native cell, enters-at rung, method) would let make_path_pog suggest the recorded rung by filename or metadata instead of by arithmetic.

What adopting this would touch

  • hazelbean/pyramids.py: pyramid_side_arcseconds, pyramid_compatible_resolution_bounds, pyramid_tile_degrees (all three candidates untiled), the shapes and geotransform dicts; the derived overview table and the tile matrix set update themselves. test_tiles.py matrix count.
  • hazelbean/pog.py: the error message fixes above.
  • docs/pogs.qmd resolution and overview tables; docs/_conventions_reference.md (its supported-set sentence still lists 7200 and 14400); manuscript A.1, A.6 and Appendix E.
  • Regeneration of every existing POG at the ten rungs 600 s divides, which the pending regeneration covers.
